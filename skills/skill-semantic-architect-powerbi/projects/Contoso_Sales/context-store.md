## 1. Industry Overview

### Industry: Retail

Retail analytics focuses on understanding consumer purchasing behavior, optimizing inventory turnover, and maximizing revenue per channel. The Contoso model covers multi-channel sales (online and brick-and-mortar), product performance, and geographic distribution across multiple markets.

### Key Analytical Challenges

- Tracking sales velocity and seasonal fluctuations across product categories and regions
- Measuring cost efficiency and gross margin at the transaction level
- Understanding customer acquisition and lifetime value trends

### Detected Business Context

The model records individual sales transactions linked to a product catalog, a customer master, a store/geography dimension, and a standard date spine. Measures exist for Sales Amount, Total Cost, and Total Quantity. The grain of FactSales is one row per order line item.

## 3. KPI Catalog

### Proposed Measures

#### Sales Amount

```dax
[Sales Amount] =
    SUMX(
        FactSales,
        FactSales[Quantity] * FactSales[UnitPrice]
    )
```

- **Justification**: Total gross revenue before any deductions. The primary top-line metric for any retail executive dashboard.
- **Business Question**: "What was our total revenue for the period?"
- **Category**: Revenue
- **Suggested Format**: `$#,0.00`

---

#### Total Cost

```dax
[Total Cost] =
    SUMX(
        FactSales,
        FactSales[Quantity] * FactSales[UnitCost]
    )
```

- **Justification**: Total procurement cost across all transactions. Required to compute gross margin.
- **Business Question**: "What did our sold inventory cost us?"
- **Category**: Cost
- **Suggested Format**: `$#,0.00`

---

#### Gross Margin

```dax
[Gross Margin] =
    [Sales Amount] - [Total Cost]
```

- **Justification**: Absolute profitability indicator before operating expenses.
- **Business Question**: "How much gross profit did we generate?"
- **Category**: Profitability
- **Suggested Format**: `$#,0.00`

---

#### Gross Margin %

```dax
[Gross Margin %] =
    DIVIDE([Gross Margin], [Sales Amount])
```

- **Justification**: Industry-standard profitability ratio. Retail benchmarks typically range from 25 % to 55 % depending on category mix.
- **Business Question**: "What percentage of revenue became gross profit?"
- **Category**: Profitability
- **Suggested Format**: `0.0%`

---

#### Total Quantity

```dax
[Total Quantity] =
    SUM(FactSales[Quantity])
```

- **Justification**: Volume metric to track units moved, independent of price effects.
- **Business Question**: "How many units did we sell?"
- **Category**: Volume
- **Suggested Format**: `#,0`

---

#### Average Order Value

```dax
[Average Order Value] =
    DIVIDE([Sales Amount], DISTINCTCOUNT(FactSales[SalesKey]))
```

- **Justification**: Measures transaction quality. Higher AOV signals effective upselling or premium product mix.
- **Business Question**: "What is the average value per transaction?"
- **Category**: Efficiency
- **Suggested Format**: `$#,0.00`

---

#### Sales YTD

```dax
[Sales YTD] =
    CALCULATE(
        [Sales Amount],
        DATESYTD(DimDate[Date])
    )
```

- **Justification**: Standard time-intelligence measure for progress-to-date tracking against annual targets.
- **Business Question**: "How much have we sold so far this year?"
- **Category**: Revenue / Time Intelligence
- **Suggested Format**: `$#,0`

---

#### Sales vs Last Year

```dax
[Sales vs Last Year] =
    CALCULATE(
        [Sales Amount],
        SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(DimDate[Date])
    )
```

- **Justification**: Enables year-over-year comparison — the most common executive benchmark in retail.
- **Business Question**: "How does current period compare to the same period last year?"
- **Category**: Growth
- **Suggested Format**: `$#,0`
